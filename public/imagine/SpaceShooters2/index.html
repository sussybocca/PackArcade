OPTIONS & CONTROLS

DESKTOP CONTROLS

W/S: main thrust · A/D: strafe · R/F: vertical thrust · Arrow keys: yaw/pitch · Q/E: roll · Space: single shot · Shift: heavy salvo · C: camera · P/Esc: pause · H: cheats

MOBILE CONTROLS

Drag the left joystick to steer and apply thrust. Tap SINGLE to fire one projectile; SALVO launches all available heavy projectiles and recharges in 40 seconds. Steering and thrust are assisted for touch usability.
